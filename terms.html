3. The Challenge & Evaluation Process

The challenge is a 2-phase simulated trading evaluation conducted on MetaTrader 5. Challenge accounts use simulated capital — no real money is traded during the evaluation. Upon passing both phases and completing KYC, the trader receives access to a funded account trading the Company's capital.

Phase 1 targets: 8% profit target, 5% max daily loss, 10% max drawdown, minimum 5 trading days, maximum 30 calendar days.

Phase 2 targets: 5% profit target, 5% max daily loss, 10% max drawdown, minimum 5 trading days, maximum 60 calendar days.

The Company reserves the right to modify evaluation parameters with 14 days' notice to existing challenge holders.

5. Funded Account & Profit Sharing

Upon passing both evaluation phases and completing KYC, the Company will provide a funded MT5 account. The trader trades the Company's capital and retains a percentage of net profits according to their challenge tier:

  • Starter ($10K) — 80% profit share
  • Standard ($25K) — 80% profit share
  • Advanced ($50K) — 85% profit share
  • Elite ($100K) — 85% profit share
  • Pro ($200K) — 90% profit share

Profit sharing applies to net realised profits only. Floating/unrealised profits are not eligible for payout.
