About IMARA

Capital, tools and community for Africa's traders

IMARA Logic Funded is a modern proprietary trading firm built to give skilled traders across Africa — and the world — real capital, a fair evaluation process, and the support to grow into professional, funded traders.

Our Story

IMARA was founded in 2021 in Nairobi, Kenya, by a small group of traders and technologists who believed that talent, not capital, should be the only barrier to becoming a professional trader. Many promising traders across the continent had the skill but lacked the funds to trade at scale — so we built a platform that bridges that gap.

Today, IMARA stands for Endure. Rise. Lead. — values that describe both the journey of our traders and the spirit of the team building the firm. We provide a transparent, two-phase evaluation, fund successful traders with up to $200,000 in simulated capital, and pay out up to 90% of profits via wire transfer, crypto, and mobile money — including M-Pesa, MTN and Orange Money.
