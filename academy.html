Know What You're Trading

Financial Markets

IMARA funded accounts give you access to a wide range of instruments on MT5. Understanding what moves each market is the first step to trading it well.

  • Forex — 40+ currency pairs (majors, minors and exotics) driven by interest rates, inflation and economic data.
  • Metals — Gold (XAU/USD) and Silver, often traded as a hedge during uncertainty or dollar weakness.
  • Indices — US30, NAS100, SPX500 and more, tracking the performance of major stock markets.
  • Commodities — Oil and Gas, sensitive to supply, demand and geopolitical events.
  • Crypto CFDs — trade price movement on major cryptocurrencies without holding the underlying asset.

Read The Charts

Technical Analysis

Technical analysis is the study of price charts to anticipate future moves. MT5 gives you 21 timeframes and 80+ indicators to build your edge.

  • Candlesticks — each candle shows the open, high, low and close for a period, and patterns (engulfing, doji, pin bars) hint at where price may go next.
  • Support & resistance — price levels where buying or selling pressure has historically reversed or paused a trend.
  • Trend & momentum indicators — moving averages, RSI and MACD help confirm the strength and direction of a move.
  • Risk-to-reward — plan your stop loss and take profit before entering, and only take trades where the potential reward justifies the risk.

Practise these tools on a free MT5 demo, available for Windows, Mac, iOS and Android.

Trade The News

Fundamental Analysis

Fundamental analysis looks at the economic and political forces that drive prices — interest rate decisions, employment data, inflation reports and central bank statements.

  • Interest rates — central bank decisions are some of the biggest market movers; higher rates often strengthen a currency.
  • Employment & inflation data — releases like Non-Farm Payrolls and CPI can trigger sharp, fast moves around their release time.
  • Risk sentiment — geopolitical events and "risk-on / risk-off" moods shift money between currencies, gold and equities.
  • Plan around the calendar — many traders reduce position size or stay flat just before high-impact releases to manage volatility.
